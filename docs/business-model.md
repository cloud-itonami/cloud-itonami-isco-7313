# Business Model: Jewellery Workshop Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7313`
- ISCO-08: `7313`
- Occupation: Jewellery and Precious Metal Workers
- Social impact: worker-safety, product-quality, custody-integrity

## Customer

- jewellery workshop operators / precious-metal fabrication contractors
- independent jewellery and precious metal working crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- precious-material/finished-piece inventory data logging
- non-precious materials/tools supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to workshop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a jewellery-fabrication-execution decision
  (e.g. deciding to proceed with specific fabrication work), ever
- no authorization of a precious-materials custody transfer, ever
- no override of a workshop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (burn exposure, chemical/acid exposure,
  custody discrepancy) always route to human sign-off, regardless of
  confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
- inventory logging is metadata-only — it never doubles as, or
  substitutes for, a custody-transfer authorization
